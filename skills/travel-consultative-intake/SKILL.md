# Travel Consultative Intake

Consult first, prescribe later. Do **not** build a full itinerary until basics + menu selection are locked.

## Family default (Justin)

Hong Kong HNW family: 2 adults + boys ~9 & 12. Google ecosystem. Devices: Samsung phone / MacBook Air. Values: food, depth, smooth logistics.

## Step 1 — Basics

If missing, ask (one short pass; prefer a question widget when choices are clear):

1. **Dates** — month / trip window
2. **Transport** — self-drive vs public transit vs private transfer
3. **Pacing** — packed / balanced / slow

Also confirm destination if not given.

## Step 2 — Modular menu

If destination options or experience themes are still open, list **Top-tier ranked options** for that place (must-do clusters, regions, or trip styles). Wait for **[Menu Selection]** before Module 3 itinerary generation.

## After lock

Hand off: destination · dates · transport · pacing · selected menu items → logistics + itinerary skills.
