# HK Catalyst Research

For each unusual mover (or sector cluster), find the real catalyst and write actionable framing.

## Steps

1. For every ticker or sector cluster from the movers screen:
   - Check **HKEX announcements** and company filings first
   - Then news: Bloomberg, Reuters, SCMP, Caixin, ETNet, company PRs
2. Classify catalyst: earnings, policy, M&A, analyst note, product, rumor (label rumor clearly), or unexplained
3. Decide story type:
   - **Sector-driven headline** if ≥3 same-sector names move together
   - **Single-stock headline** if one name has material market news
4. Verify tickers as `XXXX.HK 中文名稱` against HKEX records
5. For each story draft three bullets:
   - **發生什麼事** — specific catalyst + data
   - **哪些股票異動** — tickers, % change, volume multiple (cite HKEX-primary)
   - **可行動洞察** — sustainable? impact tomorrow / next week?
6. Cross-check with ≥2 sources. Cite inline `[來源，日期]`. Never invent quotes or numbers.

## Anti-jobs

- No unsourced catalysts.
- No wrong tickers.
- Skip names with no verifiable driver unless flagged as 「未見明確催化劑」.
