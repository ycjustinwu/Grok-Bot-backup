# YouTube 極詳細摘要

Write for a Chinese-speaking investment audience in **繁體中文**. Watch or read the video/transcript, then produce a **極詳細摘要** in the exact format below. Stay objective, dense, and on-topic — no fluff.

## Inputs

Require (ask once if missing):

1. **Video URL** (or an attached video / transcript)
2. **Topics to focus on** — optional; if omitted, cover the full chapter timeline

## How to get the content

1. Resolve metadata: title, channel, URL, upload date, duration, host names.
2. Prefer an official transcript or captions when available; otherwise watch/describe the video in order and take structured notes by chapter timestamps.
3. If only a URL is given and you cannot access it, say so plainly and ask for a transcript paste or download — do not invent quotes, numbers, or arguments.
4. Scope the notes to the requested topics when a focus list is provided.

## Output format (follow exactly)

### 影片詳細摘要

**標題：** [Video title]
**頻道：** [Channel name]
**連結：** [URL]
**上傳日期：** [年月日]
**長度：** [Duration]
**主持人：** [Host names separated by、]

> 以下為影片內容的極詳細摘要，按官方章節時間軸逐段整理，包含各主持人的主要論點、關鍵引述、數據、辯論及結論。本摘要力求客觀、全面且精準。以下只針對[TOPICS]做 summary。

(If no topic filter was given, drop the last sentence of the blockquote or write「全文」.)

---

For **each topic**, use:

### [Topic Section Title — Chinese with English term in brackets]

**[Host Name] 的觀點**
- Bullet points (`•`) for each key argument
- Include specific data, numbers, company names, and quotes when the speaker said them
- End with a 1–2 sentence synthesis of their overall stance

Repeat for each host/speaker on that topic.

**整體討論焦點及結論**
- Debate or agreement?
- Key takeaway?
- Notable quotes or memorable phrases?

End every topic section with a clear 1-line **總結**.

## Style rules

- Write in **繁體中文**
- On first use, mix English technical terms with Chinese in brackets — e.g. 護城河（moat）、年度運行率（annual run rate）
- Use 「」 for quoted terms or buzzwords
- Use `→` for cause–effect or transitions (e.g. 「Tesla 削弱 BMW 地位 → 品牌護城河貶值」)
- Bullets: concise but information-dense
- Keep PE ratios, dollar figures, percentages, and named examples whenever the speaker mentioned them
- Do not invent numbers, quotes, or stances not in the source

## Quality bar

- Highly detailed, chapter-aware, speaker-attributed
- Finance/tech media tone: precise, not hype
- If a requested topic was not discussed, say so in one line under that heading instead of padding
