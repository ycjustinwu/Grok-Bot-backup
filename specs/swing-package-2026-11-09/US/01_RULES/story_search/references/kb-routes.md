KB dated ~1H 2026 research pack. Use to pick the line, not to print today's figures. Any number,
policy progress, or flow share that conflicts with a search in the last 30 days → search wins,
mark it in 報告頭部 KB 覆蓋註記.

## A. China policy (HK)

### A1. Four transmission layers

```
[1] 中央「十五五」頂層部署
  → [2] 部委專項規劃 / 行動方案
  → [3] 地方落地指標 / 重大項目名單
  → [4] 企業補貼與採購訂單兌現  ← price-sensitive
```

Closer to layer 4 = higher certainty. Layer 1 only → narrative ≤5.

### A2. 2026 policy lines (by certainty)

| Line | Certainty | Who benefits | Why Now frame |
|------|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| AI+ 實體經濟 | 極高 | 算力、具身智能、垂直大模型、AI 芯片 | AI moves from chat to work / monetisation |
| 反內卷 / 產業重塑 | 高 | 鋼鐵、水泥、光伏、動力電池龍頭 | Capacity exit → PPI → leader margins |
| 算電協同 / 能效 | 高 | 綠色算力、數據中心節能 | Power is the AI bottleneck |
| 內需 / 投資於人 | 中高 | 服務消費、養老托育、高品質消費 | Fiscal tilt to households; check subsidy size |

### A3. Company screens

- R&D subsidy share rising (hard tech)
- Order-to-Bill stays > 1
- Writes national / industry standards (chain owner)
- 「首台套」 designation (procurement priority)
- Subsidy / revenue too high = policy dependent, weak fundamentals

### A4. HK market plumbing (search before quoting)

- Southbound holding / turnover share is core liquidity. Old KB 21% / 28% is stale. Refresh every run.
- Southbound often buys dips.
- Chinese names supply most HK profits. AH premium is a repair story, not a fact.
- Stock Connect info / settlement changes move liquidity and the valuation gap.

## D. Pharma / biotech (HK + US)

### D1. China policy seat

- 生物醫藥 = 新興支柱產業, not a generic strategic emerging industry
- STAR + HK 18C = pre-profit biotech funding rails
- Commercial-insurance innovative-drug list = high-price payment room
- FIC / BIC encouraged. Pipeline + going-out is the valuation core

### D2. Industry + going-out

- Focus — CGT, ADC, antibodies, nucleic acid, radiopharma; device import substitution (surgical robots, high-end imaging)
- License-out / NewCo = going-out 2.0. Search deal size / upfront / milestones. No amount → do not invent one
- AI for Science — discovery-cycle story. No named collab or data → strength ≤5

### D3. Required search cover (pharma names)

1. Clinical / registration — readout, NDA/BLA, priority review, indication
2. BD — license-out / in, NewCo, milestones
3. Payment / policy — NRD, commercial list, VBP, FDA / CDE
4. HK — 18C / 18A status, southbound, cornerstone, lock-up

## C. Narrative quality

### C1. Why Now (must answer)

1. What just changed in market / tech / regulation?
2. Why did the old attempt fail, and why does it work now?
3. How long is the window? Cost of waiting?

### C2. Seven moats

Network, scale, switching cost, reverse-position, scarce resource, brand, process power.

Pharma map — patent / clinical barrier, channel, process know-how, RWE, physician switching cost.

### C3. Strength 1–10

| Score | Bar | Stage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| 8–10 | Early data + unit economics / orders / upfront verified | Probable |
| 4–7 | Tight logic, no hard data yet | Plausible |
| 1–3 | Only 「maybe」 | Possible |

EARNINGS PASS and no same-week hard catalyst → cap 6.

### C4. Five traps (name and deduct)

- Narrative fallacy (random attribution)
- Big-market illusion (TAM only)
- Internal contradiction (high growth + low risk + low reinvestment)
- Survivorship bias
- Fairy tale (no financial constraint)
